# humanity wall — article pack

This package contains 15 publishable first-draft articles for `humanitywall.org/read`.

## what is included

- 15 standalone Markdown articles with YAML front matter
- `article-index.csv` for editorial/dev handoff
- `source-map.md` with research and community-source notes
- `publish-checklist.md` with voice, privacy, E-E-A-T and safety checks

## editorial approach

These drafts are grounded, where useful, in patterns from public online discussions (especially public Reddit communities around venting, unsent letters, grief, confessions and random thoughts) plus primary/peer-reviewed research where a factual claim needs support.

They do **not** reproduce real wall notes. Community posts are used as research material for recurring themes, not as content inventory. Examples inside the articles are original/composited.

The drafts deliberately avoid promising that venting, confessing or writing will heal, cure, treat, create closure, or improve health. The research on expressive writing is mixed; the articles frame writing as expression, not medicine.

## before publishing

Each article should receive a final human edit for house style and exact product behavior. Confirm every wall URL, privacy statement, moderation statement and crisis resource immediately before launch. Do not add invented authorship or clinical review.

Where a community source is linked in the research section, consider whether you want those links public on the final page. They are included here mainly for editorial provenance; a production version can cite the underlying research/organizations while keeping community-source notes in an internal research document.
